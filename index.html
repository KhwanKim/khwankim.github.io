I study how producers and organizations create novelty, how audiences recognize and evaluate it, and how it evolves and spreads across creative industries. I also study how social networks form—how strangers perceive one another, connect, and become friends.

Music, film, books and theatre. Computational methods & quasi-experimental designs.
