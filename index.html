I’m an assistant professor in Organisations & Innovation at UCL School of Management.

I study how people and organisations create new things, and how audiences come to value them. Much of my work is in music, film and publishing. I’m also interested in how social networks form, particularly how strangers connect and become friends.

Before joining UCL, I completed my PhD at INSEAD and an MA in Sociology at the University of Chicago.
